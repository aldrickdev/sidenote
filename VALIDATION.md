## Manual checks still worth doing in your Chrome profile

The automated suite loads extension pages at panel widths and runs the real worker. It does not drive the browser's native toolbar/side-panel UI or approve live website-permission prompts. Title fetch network/permission behavior is unit-tested with mocked responses; third-party site compatibility is not claimed.

1. Load `dist/`, pin the icon, and click it on a regular web page. Verify the native panel opens and **Save current tab** copies the URL/title.
2. Switch to an unrelated website and save again directly from the open panel, without clicking the toolbar icon again.
3. Paste a new URL, approve its optional site-access prompt, and verify the fetched title. Try denying access too; saving without a title must remain available.
4. Open a bookmark and verify your actual Chrome side panel remains visible while its destination becomes the active tab.
5. Export a real backup before moving the unpacked extension directory, changing profiles, or uninstalling it.
